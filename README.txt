Five-year walking prediction calculator (English offline version)

1. Download and extract the ZIP package.
2. Open Calculator.html in Chrome or Edge.
3. Enter the clinical information and select Calculate probability.
4. The result and individual SHAP explanation appear on the same page.
5. No R installation or internet connection is required. Editing an input clears the old result.

Download the file before opening it; email or messaging-app previews may not run calculations.
If the motor score is not testable or unknown, select its checkbox. Do not enter codes 888 or 999.

Model: GBM_1208_20260926; 468 training, 117 internal holdout and 623 earlier-year validation patients.
Uses the original fitted model, fixed imputation values and probability-scale interventional SHAP.
Outcome: completing at least one of three walking tasks at post-injury year 5; assistive devices permitted.
For research demonstration only; this is not independent-walking probability. No clinical action threshold is established.
Inputs are not uploaded or stored. No patient identifiers or original data workbooks are included.
