Five-year walking prediction calculator

NSCISC 2021 public database | Final GBM | Offline version

Use after initial inpatient rehabilitation baseline data collection is complete. The outcome is the ability to walk 150 feet at home, walk one street block outside, or walk up one flight of steps at post-injury year 5. Completing at least one task counts as a positive outcome; assistive devices are permitted.

Clinical inputs

Total days hospitalized in the Model System acute/subacute medical unit.

Major associated injury from the same trauma, as defined by NSCISC.

Unknown values are imputed using the training-set median or mode. Acute medical unit stay and vertebral injury status must be known, together with at least one acute neurologic assessment.

Variable definitions and model information

The model was fitted in 468 training patients and evaluated in 117 internal holdout patients and 623 earlier-year validation patients. This calculator uses the same final model without refitting or recalibration.

    Associated major injury includes moderate or severe traumatic brain injury (GCS ≤12), nonvertebral fractures requiring surgery, severe facial injuries involving sensory organs, major chest injuries requiring a chest tube or mechanical ventilation, traumatic or surgically required limb amputation, severe hemorrhage, brachial plexus injury, and visceral injuries requiring surgery, arising from the same trauma.

    For research demonstration only. This probability is not the probability of independent walking; no clinical action threshold has been established. Inputs are processed in this browser without being uploaded or stored. Calculations also work offline.
